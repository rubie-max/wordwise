What your own words use today

Even more realistic, but too big for a phone

These need a strong computer, so they can't run inside WordWise. Their free online demos let you type a line and listen. They can be slow or busy, and some may ask you to wait in a queue.

  • Chatterbox: very natural, and can copy a voice from a short recording.
  • F5-TTS: copies the voice and accent of a sample clip.
  • Dia: made for lively conversation, with laughs and sighs.
  • TTS Arena: type a line and hear two open-source voices side by side, without knowing which is which.
  • Kokoro's own demo: the same voices as above, made on a fast server.
  • Piper samples: a short recording of every Piper voice.
